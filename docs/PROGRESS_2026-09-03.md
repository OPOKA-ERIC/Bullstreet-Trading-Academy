## Client Decisions (confirmed 09 September 2026)

### Domain
- **Chosen:** bullstreetacademy.com
- **Registrar:** Namecheap (~$12/year)
- **Status:** PENDING — client needs to buy the domain

### Website Hosting
- **Chosen:** Vercel Pro ($20/month) OR Namecheap Shared Hosting (Stellar, ~$6/month)
- **Note:** Vercel free tier is NOT allowed for commercial sites. Pro plan required.
- **Status:** PENDING — needs domain purchase first

### Video Hosting
- **Chosen:** Cloudflare Stream
- **Pricing:** $5/month minimum + $5 per 1,000 minutes stored + $1 per 1,000 minutes delivered
- **Reason:** Cheapest option, private videos, only paying students can watch
- **Status:** PENDING — needs Cloudflare account

### Payment Provider
- **Chosen:** Flutterwave (NOT Stripe, NOT Paystack)
- **Reason:** Stripe doesn't support Uganda. Paystack doesn't operate in Uganda. Flutterwave is licensed by Bank of Uganda (August 2024) and supports MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Visa, Mastercard.
- **Fees:** ~1.4% per mobile money transaction, ~3.5% per international card transaction
- **Setup cost:** Free (no monthly fee, no setup fee — only % per sale)
- **Status:** PENDING — client needs to create Flutterwave business account

### Logo
- **Status:** DEFERRED — client will handle transparent background conversion later (remove.bg or designer)
